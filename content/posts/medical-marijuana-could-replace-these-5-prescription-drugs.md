A [new study](http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/dar.12323/abstract) published in the journal “***Drug and Alcohol Review***” found that people are increasingly substituting prescription medication, alcohol, and illicit drugs with [cannabis](http://www.attn.com/stories/2116/reason-marijuana-illegal-united-states), signaling a shift in the public’s understanding of marijuana’s medicinal benefits. Of the 473 adult cannabis users surveyed by the **Centre for Addictions Research of British Columbia**,more than 86 percent of respondents reported giving up one or more of the substances for pot, with the majority (80.6 percent) [forgoing pharmaceuticals for the non-toxic alternative](http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/dar.12323/abstract).

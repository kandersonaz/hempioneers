***State Decriminalization Developments***

Oregon is moving a step closer to an active adult-use cannabis industry. Though possession and home-cultivation was legalized on July 1, Oregon has not issued any licenses for adult-use dispensaries and likely won’t until next year. The state will, however, [allow medical dispensaries](http://salveocapital.us11.list-manage1.com/track/click?u=ea83b65b4c82d0cf8741ffc4d&id=3c26e85322&e=38caa72246) to sell marijuana to adults starting on October 1.

[![legal and decriminalized marijuans state map](http://hempioneers.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/legal-and-decriminalized-marijuans-state-map-1024x683.jpg)](/media/2015/08/legal-and-decriminalized-marijuans-state-map.jpg)

An outline for eventual adult-use legalization of marijuana in California [was released by a working group](http://salveocapital.us11.list-manage2.com/track/click?u=ea83b65b4c82d0cf8741ffc4d&id=943ede0329&e=38caa72246) overseen by the state’s Lieutenant Governor. It calls for comprehensive, strict regulation which is a stark but welcome contrast from the current largely unregulated medical system which does a poor job defining allowed practicing and protecting businesses from being shut down by local governments.

***Federal Law Developments***

Long-time anti-drug warrior New York Senator Chuck Schumer has [come on as a sponsor](http://salveocapital.us11.list-manage.com/track/click?u=ea83b65b4c82d0cf8741ffc4d&id=ee91d65d55&e=38caa72246) of the CARERS Act – a bill to reschedule marijuana to Schedule 2, and essentially legalize medical cannabis in the U.S. This came mere days before New York State [awarded five medical cannabis licenses](http://salveocapital.us11.list-manage.com/track/click?u=ea83b65b4c82d0cf8741ffc4d&id=ab3c937c44&e=38caa72246). These five licenses each allow for one cultivation/processing site and up to four dispensaries.[.](/buying-fluconazole-craigslist)

<p>Individuals with multiple sclerosis (MS) tend to have their first signs between ages 20 and 40. Usually the symptoms get better, but then come back. Some may come and go, while others linger.<br />
<a name="1"></a></p>
<h3>Early Symptoms of MS</h3>
<ul type="disc">
<li>Loss of balance</li>
<li>Numbness</li>
<li>Tingling</li>
<li>Weakness in an arm or leg</li>
<li>Blurred or double vision</li>
<li>Thinking problems</li>
</ul>

<p>Hempioneers is an Arizona based digital media venture. Our operations are 100% legal throughout the USA.</p>
<p>Our mission is to provide reliable fact based information on the medical, nutritional and business benefits of hemp, cannabis, marijuana and their many derived compounds and extracts for industrial and consumer uses.</p>
<p>Once open, our estore will offer premium pay per view content from industry experts, and a small selection of the highest quality fully legal products for sale/distribution in all 50 US states.</p>

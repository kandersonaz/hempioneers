<p>Asthma and Breathing Disorders Summary</p>
<p>Asthma is a condition in which your airways swell and produce extra mucus. This leads to breathing being cumbersome and can trigger coughing, wheezing and shortness of breath.</p>
<p>For some people, asthma is a minor nuisance. For others, it can be a major problem that interferes with daily activities and may lead to a life-threatening asthma attack.</p>
<p>Asthma can&#8217;t be cured and asthma often changes over time, it&#8217;s important to work with your doctor and track your  symptoms and adjust treatment as needed.</p>

<div class="editortext">Arthritis and Cannabis Positive Effects and Supporting Scientific Journals</div>
<div class="editortext"></div>
<div class="editortext"><i>Rheumatology</i> reported in a Jan. 2006 article titled &#8220;Preliminary Assessment of The Efficacy, Tolerability and Safety of A Cannabis-based Medicine (Sativex) in The Treatment of Pain Caused By Rheumatoid Arthritis,&#8221; by D.R. Blake et al.:</div>
<blockquote>
<div class="editortext">
<p align="justify">&#8220;In comparison with placebo, the CBM [cannabis-based medicine] produced statistically significant improvements in pain on movement, pain at rest, quality of sleep&#8230;.</p>
<p align="justify">In the first ever controlled trial of a CBM in RA [rheumatoid arthritis], a significant analgesic effect was observed and disease activity was significantly suppressed following Sativex treatment.&#8221;</p>
</div>

<div class="editortext">The <i>Journal of Neuroimmunology</i>, in their Sep. 2005 article titled &#8220;Cannabinoids and the Immune System: Potential For The Treatment of Inflammatory Diseases?&#8221; by J. Ludovic and Takashi Yamamura, stated:</div>
<blockquote>
<div class="editortext">
<p align="justify">&#8220;Studies from chronic cannabis smokers have provided much of the evidence for immunomodulatory [modifying or regulating the immune system] effects of cannabis in humans&#8230;</p>
<p align="justify">Cannabinoids can modulate both the function and secretion of cytokines [regulatory proteins]from immune cells.</p>
<p align="justify">Therefore, cannabinoids may be considered for treatment of inflammatory disease.&#8221;</p>

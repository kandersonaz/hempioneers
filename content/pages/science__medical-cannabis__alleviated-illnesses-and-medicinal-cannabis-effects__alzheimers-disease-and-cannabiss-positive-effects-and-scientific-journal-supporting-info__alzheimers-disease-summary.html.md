<p><strong>Alzheimer’s Disease Summary</strong></p>
<p>Alzheimer&#8217;s disease is a disease which increases its effect over time that destroys memory and important mental functions.</p>
<p>It&#8217;s the most common cause of dementia and it&#8217;s changes are severe and interfere with day-to-day life.</p>
<p>In Alzheimer&#8217;s disease, the brain cells themselves degenerate and die, causing a steady decline in over all mental function.</p>
<p>There&#8217;s no cure for Alzheimer&#8217;s disease, it&#8217;s important to seek supportive services and tap into your support network as early as possible.</p>

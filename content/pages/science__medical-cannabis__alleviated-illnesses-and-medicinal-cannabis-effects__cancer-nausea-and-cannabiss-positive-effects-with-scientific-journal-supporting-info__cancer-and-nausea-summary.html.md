<section id="_2">
<h3 id="_3">Nausea and Vomiting are distressing typical results of cancer therapy and medicines.</h3>
<p id="_200" tabindex="0">Nausea and Vomiting are distressing typical results of cancer therapy and medicines.</p>
<p id="_4" tabindex="0">Nausea is an unpleasant wavelike feeling in the back of the stomach and throat that will likely lead to vomiting.</p>
<p tabindex="0">Vomiting is throwing up the contents of the stomach through the mouth.</p>
<p tabindex="0">Retching is the movement of the stomach and esophagus without vomiting and is also called dry heaves. Although treatments have improved, nausea and vomiting are still serious side effects of cancer therapy.</p>
</section>
<section id="_127">
<h3 id="_128">Nausea and vomiting must be controlled so that the patient can continue treatment and have a better quality of life.</h3>
<p id="_187" tabindex="0">It is very important to prevent and control nausea and vomiting in patients with cancer, so that they can continue treatment and perform activities of daily life. Nausea and vomiting that are not controlled can cause the following:</p>
<div class="pdq-content-list">
<ul id="_185">
<li>Chemical changes in the body.</li>
<li>Mental changes.</li>
<li>Loss of appetite.</li>
<li>Malnutrition.</li>
<li>Dehydration.</li>
<li>A torn esophagus.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<p id="_130" tabindex="0">There are many types of nausea and vomiting that are caused by cancer therapy:</p>
<div class="pdq-content-list">
<ul id="_131">
<li>Delayed.</li>
<li>Breakthrough.</li>
<li>Chronic</li>
<li>Acute</li>
</ul>
</div>
<p id="_188" tabindex="0"><strong>Acute nausea and vomiting</strong>: Nausea and vomiting that happen within 24 hours after beginning chemotherapy.</p>
<p id="_133" tabindex="0"><strong>Delayed nausea and vomiting</strong>: Nausea and vomiting that happen more than 24 hours after chemotherapy. Also called late nausea and vomiting.</p>
<p id="_201" tabindex="0"><strong>Breakthrough nausea and vomiting</strong>: Nausea and vomiting that happen within 5 days after getting antinausea treatment.</p>
<p id="_184" tabindex="0"><strong>Chronic nausea and vomiting</strong>: In patients with more serious cancer, chronic nausea and vomiting may be caused by radiation therapy. Other causes of chronic nausea and vomiting include the following:</p>
<div class="pdq-content-list">
<ul id="_186">
<li>Brain Tumors or pressure on the brain.</li>
<li>Stomach ulcers.</li>
<li>Dehydration.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</section>
<section id="_206">
<h3 id="_207">Overall healthcare costs are higher for patients with nausea and vomiting that is not controlled.</h3>
<p id="_208" tabindex="0">Healthcare costs for patients with severe nausea and vomiting caused by cancer therapy may be higher due to:</p>
<div class="pdq-content-list">
<ul id="_209">
<li>Longer hospital stays.</li>
<li>Need for help with everyday activities.</li>
<li>Lost work hours.</li>
<li>Depression</li>
</ul>
</div>

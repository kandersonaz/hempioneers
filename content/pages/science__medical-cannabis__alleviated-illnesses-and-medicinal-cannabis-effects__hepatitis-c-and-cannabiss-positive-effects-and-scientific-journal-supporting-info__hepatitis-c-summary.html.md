<p>Hepatitis C Summary</p>
<p>Hepatitis C is a liver infection caused by the Hep, C virus (HCV). Hepatitis C is a blood-borne virus. Today, most people become infected with the Hepatitis C virus by sharing needles or other equipment to inject drugs. For some people, hepatitis C is a short illness but for 70%–85% of those infected with Hepatitis C, it becomes a chronic infection.</p>
<p>Chronic Hepatitis C is a serious disease than can result in long-term health problems, even death. The majority of infected persons might not be aware of their infection because they are not clinically ill. There is no vaccine for Hepatitis C.

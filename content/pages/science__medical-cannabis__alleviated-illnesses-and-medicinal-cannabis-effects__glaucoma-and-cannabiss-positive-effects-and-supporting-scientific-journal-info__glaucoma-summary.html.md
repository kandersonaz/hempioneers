<p>Glaucoma Summary</p>
<p>Glaucoma is a group of eye conditions that damage the optic nerve. This damage is often caused by an abnormally high pressure in your eye.</p>
<p>Glaucoma is one of the leading causes of blindness in the United States. It can occur at any age but is more common in older adults.</p>
<p>The most common form of glaucoma has no warning signs. The effect is so gradual that you may not notice a change in vision until the condition is at an advanced stage.</p>

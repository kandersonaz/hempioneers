<p>Arthritis Disease Summary</p>
<p>Arthritis is inflammation of your joints. Symptoms of arthritis are joint pain and stiffness, which typically worsen with age.</p>
<p>The two most common types of arthritis are osteoarthritis and rheumatoid.</p>
<ol>
<li>Rheumatoid arthritis is an autoimmune disorder that first targets the lining of joints (synovium).</li>
<li>Osteoarthritis causes cartilage — the hard, slippery tissue that covers the ends of bones where they form a     joint — to break down.</li>
</ol>
<p>Uric acid crystals, infections or underlying disease, such as psoriasis or lupus, can cause other types of arthritis.</p>
<p>Treatments vary depending on the type of arthritis.</p>

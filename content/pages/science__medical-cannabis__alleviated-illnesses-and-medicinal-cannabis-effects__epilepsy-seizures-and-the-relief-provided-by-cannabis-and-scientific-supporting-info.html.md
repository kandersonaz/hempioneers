<p>Epilepsy, Seizures and the relief provided by Cannabis</p>
<div class="editortext">John Brust, MD, Professor of Clinical Neurology at Columbia University, et al., concluded in their 1990 study &#8220;Illicit Drug Use and the Risk of New-onset Seizures,&#8221; published in the<i>American Journal of Epidemiology</i>:</div>
<blockquote>
<div class="editortext">
<p>&#8220;Marijuana use appeared to be a protective factor against first seizures in men. [&#8230;]Marijuana contains numerous cannabinoid compounds that differ in convulsant and anticonvulsant properties. Anticonvulsant properties were first noted in the 15th century, yet studies in humans have been few. [&#8230;]</p>
<p>The authors concluded that Marijuana use is a protective factor for new-onset seizures.&#8221;</p>

<p class="node">Epilepsy is a general term for the tendency to have seizures.</p>
<p class="node">When identifiable, the source an individual has epilepsy usually involves some form of injury to the brain. For most people, though, epilepsy&#8217;s causes aren&#8217;t known.</p>
<p class="node"><a name="1"></a>Seizures and Epilepsy</p>
<p class="node">A seizure occurs when a surge of electrical impulses in the brain. They spread to neighboring areas and The electrical impulses can be transmitted to the muscles, causing uncontrollable twitches or convulsions.</p>
